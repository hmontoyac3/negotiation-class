What worked

Finding the interest

Any team that asked "what do you need from today to feel it was worth it?" found the unlock immediately. That's principle 2: focus on interests, not positions.

What didn't work

Arguing position

Teams that said "we're tired," "we've learned enough," "let us leave" were arguing their position — not addressing mine. I had no reason to move.

The win-win

The deal that was always there

You commit to something that proves learning. I get my evidence. You get your exit. Both get 100% of what you actually needed. Same as the oranges.

The meta-lesson

It's always the same question

Salary. Oranges. A classroom exit. In every negotiation: "What do you actually need — and why?" That question is worth more than any tactic.

Three modules. One day. One insight that runs through all of it:
Teams work when people understand each other's actual needs — not just their stated roles.
