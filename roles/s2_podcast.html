🧪 BIOTECH COMPANY
Developing Dermacell skin repair cream · Needs citrus rind extract · No juice processing capabilities
Your Situation
  • A small farm in Valencia is selling its entire harvest of rare Ugli oranges — only 2,000 units, grown once a year
  • These oranges contain a unique aromatic compound your R&D team has been working with for 18 months
  • The farmer is selling the full batch to a single buyer — no splitting, or so you think
  • You and FreshPress are the two interested buyers — you need to negotiate who gets the harvest and at what price
  • Your BATNA: a synthetic substitute — it works, but it's 3x more expensive and takes 6 extra months to produce
Your Budget — Keep This Secret
€75,000 Max Budget Approved by management for this deal
€50,000 Target Price What you'd like to pay
Critical — What Your Company Actually Needs

PharmaOne extracts a compound from the rind and zest of the orange — the outer peel. Your lab strips and processes the skin. The juice and pulp are discarded immediately. You have zero use for the juice. But you haven't thought to mention this, because you assumed the other side wants the same thing you do.

⚠ Do Not Reveal
  • Your budget ceiling of €75,000
  • That the synthetic substitute exists — it shows you have an alternative and weakens your position
